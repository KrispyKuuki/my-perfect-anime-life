How this one is different

Most type tests round you off. If you land at 51 percent on a dimension they hand you the same answer as someone at 95 percent, which is why so many people get a different result the second time they take one.

This test keeps your real scores underneath and shows them to you. If a thread lands near the middle we say so, and we tell you which character you very nearly were. Uncertainty is part of your result, not something hidden from you.

Nothing you type leaves your device. There is no account, no email, no database, and no analytics. Close the tab and it is gone.
